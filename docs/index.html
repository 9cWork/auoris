Auoris on your iPhone

There's no App Store listing — instead, your phone talks directly to your PC over Wi-Fi, and installs like a real app icon.

  1. 1. On your PC, open the profile menu → Phone app, and switch on "Allow phone access".
  2. 2. Scan the QR code with your iPhone's camera — it opens Auoris in Safari, already paired.
  3. 3. Tap the Share icon in Safari, then "Add to Home Screen". It now behaves like an installed app.
  4. 4. Your phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi as your PC each time you use it — it's talking straight to your computer, not a server.
