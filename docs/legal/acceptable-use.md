# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everything you do with Auoris: messages, files, profiles, servers, GIFs, the Minecraft features and any mods or tools you connect. It is part of the Terms of Service.

## You may not

- Break the law, or help someone else to.
- Harass, threaten, stalk, bully or encourage violence or self-harm against anyone, or share hateful content that attacks people for who they are.
- Share sexual content involving minors, or sexual content that anyone has not consented to. We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities and close the account.
- Share extremely graphic violence, or content that promotes terrorism or violent extremism.
- Share other people's private information (such as home addresses, phone numbers, passwords or private photos) without their consent.
- Impersonate other people, staff or organisations, or pretend a message comes from Auoris.
- Send spam, scams, phishing, fake giveaways, mass unsolicited messages or invite floods, or run bots or webhooks to do so.
- Upload malware, or content designed to break or take over other people's devices, accounts or the Service. Files are scanned and unsafe ones are removed.
- Try to break, probe or overload the Service, bypass rate limits, upload limits or bans, or access accounts, data or systems that aren't yours.
- Evade a ban or suspension by making new accounts.
- Share content you don't have the right to share, including pirated software or copyrighted work you don't have permission for.
- Use the Minecraft features to cheat on servers that forbid it, or to harm other players' games.
- Use the AI features to produce content that breaks this policy, or to attack other people's systems.
- Buy, sell or trade accounts, or use Plus perks you did not pay for.

## Servers and moderators

People who run servers are responsible for their communities and must follow this policy. Moderators can remove content and members from their own servers. We may act on a server, or on the people who run it, if it breaks this policy.

## Reporting

You can report a message or a person from inside the app and on the website. To report something that needs urgent attention, contact {{contact}}.

## What happens if the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is we may remove content, warn you, limit features such as uploading, suspend or close the account, remove a server, and report to the authorities where the law or safety requires it. You can ask us to review a decision by contacting {{contact}}.
