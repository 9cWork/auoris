# Copyright and Content Removal

We respect other people's intellectual property and expect Auoris users to do the same. This page explains how to ask us to remove content that infringes your rights, and how to respond if your content is removed.

## Reporting infringing content

If you believe something on Auoris infringes your copyright, send a notice to {{contact}} with:

1. Your name and contact details.
2. A description of the work you say is infringed.
3. Where the content is on Auoris (a link, a server and channel name, or a username and the time it was sent) so we can find it.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
6. Your signature (a typed name is fine).

We may remove or disable the content, tell the person who posted it, and share your notice (without your contact details where we can) with them.

## If your content was removed

If you think content was removed by mistake, contact {{contact}} with the same details and a statement that you believe the content was removed in error or that you have the right to share it. If the person who made the original notice does not take legal action within a reasonable time, we may restore it.

## Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe other people's rights.
